    Table talk

    Words you will hear once the first tile or card is down.

    Boneyard
    The face-down tiles you draw from when you cannot play.
    Double
    A tile with the same number on both halves, such as 4|4. It goes across the chain.
    Blocked
    A round where nobody can play. The lowest pip total wins.
    Book
    Four cards of the same rank in Go Fish.
    Meld
    A set or a run laid on the table in Rummy.
    Stock
    The face-down pile you draw from in card games.

    Rules follow the versions most families play. House rules differ from place to place, so agree on yours before the first tile.
